## Fortalezas

- A discovery becomes a reusable voice without spending the keepsake. The four-voice limit gives the collection a small, playable ensemble rather than merely a trophy wall.
- Spatial placement has real compositional consequences: horizontal position selects a beat; vertical position selects degree and register. Release after dragging plays the moved voice immediately. This is a convincing toy-to-instrument connection in the implementation.
- Revisiting a voice in different weather, light or gestures adds interpretations, and technique selection is durable with rollback after failed storage. These variants alter envelopes, brightness, noise and pitch motion rather than being cosmetic names.
- A saved, diverse ensemble can generate a fifth small musical organism which follows the player. That is a meaningful emergence reward attached to combining roles. Foreground note priority and bounded polyphony support reliable play technically.

## Hallazgos y límites

- The card cannot be auditioned where its musical use is chosen. Its primary actions add it to the garden or start an atlas search; changing interpretation only updates the saved choice and button text. A player must travel to the house to compare the sound of a newly selected interpretation. This is a verified UI limitation, not a claim that playback is broken.
- The garden reduces six advertised roles to four. Detail and Transformation both become lead; their special character is replaced by melody in the garden. The UI acknowledges this, so it is an expressive ceiling rather than an undisclosed bug.
