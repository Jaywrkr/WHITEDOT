## Fortalezas

- Musical teaching has a concrete call-and-response form: each place sings its counted steps for two bars, and the same Line anticipates and lights those steps.
- Success has several connected expressions: a theme note, a gold Line peak, scheduled light and vibration, and a growing cube frame. Output latency and finger-down timing receive explicit treatment.
- The World rewards timing positively: an off-time crystal retains its base value; on-time play doubles it, and an eight-hit streak triples it. The pickup displays the multiplier.
- Vacío turns rhythm into an authored spatial memory: release on the first beat, move to give each star room, and hear the released note return a bar later. This is a strong rule-to-image idea.

## Hallazgos y límites

- A valid musical beat can advance the theme while Vacío returns wait or crowded and makes no chapter progress. The implementation intentionally distinguishes these outcomes through white marks and suppressed landing feedback, but whether players understand the two meanings of success is untested. This is not classified as a rules defect.
- The only rhythm guide sits at 80% of screen height, while the cube is near the center; counted marks start at only two pixels wide and five pixels high. In the supplied 390-pixel screenshots the rhythm guide is substantially less prominent than nearby world objects. Precise reading requires attention away from the cube.
