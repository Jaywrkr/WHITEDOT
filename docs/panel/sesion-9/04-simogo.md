## Fortalezas

- The opening's small cube, black field and thin line remain recognizable in Vacío and the ring journey. The song screen translates earned places into coloured circles and remaining places into dashed circles without enlarging the protagonist.
- The chapter sequence assigns Tormenta a higher energy floor and Vacío a deliberately low ceiling. Journey finale construction recalls saved chapter notes and timing, then the theme; the later eleven-place finale stages layer entrances, personal notes, a bar of silence, theme and credits. This is strong structural evidence of an authored arc, without establishing the audible result.

## Hallazgos y límites

- The six undiscovered place names on the song screen become difficult to read: their dashed rings remain discernible, while names such as Faros, Cristales and Jardín nearly disappear into the dark ground. This weakens the finale's invitation to the next six places.
- After two seconds the Journey finale assigns the entire overlay a click handler that immediately finishes it. The visible 'Explorar a pie' button appears only with the credits. A tap intended to investigate the animated chapter rings can therefore skip the personal reprise before an exit control has been shown.
