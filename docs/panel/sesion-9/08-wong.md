## Fortalezas

- The small white cube holds a clean focal point amid substantial negative space. The world feels intimate and quiet on a narrow screen.
- Lugares communicates a useful next destination twice, with a strong white continuation button and comfortable touch targets. Progress and earned stars share a consistent gold language.
- Faros turns navigation into a readable spatial metaphor: gold harbour, visible reach and edge arrows. The symbols explain more through placement than a dense HUD.
- Explicit help is contextual: an active lesson supplies steps and a current hint, with a garden gesture replay. This is a useful route back from uncertainty.

## Hallazgos y límites

- The garden's actionable geometry and ambient marks are both extremely faint. In the supplied mobile frame the cube is clear, but the nearby rings and flower shapes offer little contrast hierarchy, so identifying the next useful interaction depends heavily on prior learning.
- Quiet journey screens suppress the contextual text surfaces, while the explicit game guide is inside the folded Más section. The start frame offers Pulso beside a crystal, but no visible movement instruction. This creates a costly recovery path if the gesture has been forgotten.
- Lugares lists eleven destinations in an independently scrolling region capped at 52vh. In the supplied frame only five completed rows and Jardín are visible, with no obvious indication that further destinations continue below.
