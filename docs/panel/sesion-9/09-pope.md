## Fortalezas

- Progression and spending have distinct jobs: completing the preceding place opens the next; stars do not need to be spent. The objective exposes places completed and stars separately.
- The menu has a clear primary layer and folds secondary systems into Más. Favorite editing and sharing are also folded beneath the return action.
- Round rewards and spark purchases protect accounting on failed persistence through rollback; the ledger exposes earned and spent totals.

## Hallazgos y límites

- The round card can display 75 % a tiempo while denying the rhythm star: 74.6% rounds to 75 in the card, but the threshold uses the unrounded share. The card also omits the 75% requirement, so this boundary reads as inconsistent accounting.
- The result timer wraps seconds before carrying into minutes. At 59,999 ms it displays 0:00; the same mismatch recurs just before subsequent minute boundaries.
