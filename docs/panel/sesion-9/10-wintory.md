## Fortalezas

- The Journey ending has a composed arc: recalled chapter phrases accumulate supporting layers, yield to a restrained theme, fade, and leave two seconds before credits. The visual clock follows audible audio time.

## Hallazgos y límites

- The Journey arrangement can erase distinctions in a player's melody: every recalled pitch is forced onto one of three current chord tones. A small pure-function probe maps input MIDI 61, 62, and 63 to the same output 61 at the same first-chapter onset. This proves a loss of pitch identity; its perceptual importance remains unlistened.
- The eleven-place ending pads its duration by continuing the layers after the theme, including the Faros melody; at the final transport boundary it stops adding events without an authored gain taper. Existing notes can decay, so this is an ending-shape limitation rather than a verified click.
