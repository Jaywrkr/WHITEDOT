Description

A cube explores an open universe where every region sounds different. It finds eleven places, each farther away, and plays their rounds in time. The music answers what it does.

No long tutorial: the world is open from the first second.

Hold, and the scanner shows what is near.

Over eighty pieces for the cube and a Refuge that travels with it. Sparks are only earned by playing.
